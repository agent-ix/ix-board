## Summary

Ticket: SWM-261. PR ix-board#103 at head 90f15ec8d93bd5e5d7a2ad6596f6292e1f7f7179. Reviewed the ledger writers, v3 migration and views against FR-039, the dev-team role specs on origin/main and relay-core at the pinned rev. Defects were reproduced with temporary ledgers and a throwaway local redis-server; nothing touched the live relay or a live ledger.

## Verdict

**FAIL** - the relay joins never match real receipts, one expired order blocks assignment completion, completion rewrites historical rows, several role metrics report wrong or fabricated values, and four existing tests fail. Not mergeable.
